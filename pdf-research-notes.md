# Research notes for EdgePark Estate guides

Sources reviewed:

1. Investor.gov / U.S. SEC, Diversification: https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/investing-basics/glossary/diversification
   Key point: diversification spreads money among investments so weakness in one may be offset by others; the guides should distinguish risk reduction from risk elimination.

2. National Association of REALTORS®, Marketing: https://www.nar.realtor/marketing
   Key point: effective property marketing is not simply publishing everywhere; it focuses on target audiences, a clear professional story, identity, website, social profiles, and testimonials. The guides should cover positioning, audience segmentation, listing quality, trust, and follow-up.

Additional drafting principles:
- Include Nigeria-aware examples, but label legal, tax, financing, title, and regulatory points as jurisdiction-dependent.
- Emphasize independent legal, valuation, survey, engineering, and tax advice before a purchase or investment decision.
- Avoid promises of guaranteed returns or guaranteed profit.
- Include checklists, worksheets, formulas, case studies, action plans, and source notes.
- Treat owner verification as a process requiring official registry searches, matching identities, title review, survey/plan review, encumbrance and litigation checks, authority checks, and formal legal documentation.
- Use “illustrative example” labels for all numerical scenarios.
