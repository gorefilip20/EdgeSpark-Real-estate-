Frequently Asked Questions

How does the JV partnership model work?

You fund the acquisition and renovation. EdgeSpark handles sourcing, verification, renovation management, and resale. Profits are split according to your tier (60-80% to you). You approve every deal before funds move.

What is the minimum investment?

Our Bronze tier starts at $5,000. This lets you participate in a single deal and experience the process before committing more capital. We encourage starting small and building trust.

How are properties verified?

Every property undergoes title verification at the Land Registry, physical inspection, and a complete Deal Analyzer report showing acquisition costs, renovation estimates, and projected resale values.

What is the typical deal cycle?

Most deals complete in 2-4 months from acquisition to resale. During this period you receive regular updates with photos, progress reports, and financial tracking.

Can I visit properties before investing?

Platinum partners receive on-site property visits as part of their tier. All partners receive detailed video walkthroughs and photo documentation before any commitment.
