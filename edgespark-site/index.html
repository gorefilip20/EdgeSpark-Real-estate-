The Business Model

How It Works — In Four Clear Steps

Our model is simple, repeatable, and designed to protect your capital at every stage. You approve every deal individually before any money moves.

1

Find

Source underpriced properties from motivated sellers across Nigerian cities — our networks span Enugu, Anambra, Abia, and surrounding states.

2

Verify

Full title verification at the Land Registry with a qualified lawyer. Confirms legal ownership and checks for encumbrances. No money moves until it's clear.

3

Improve

Light cosmetic upgrades — paint, gate repairs, landscaping, plumbing — to boost perceived value and speed up the sale.

4

Resell

Sell the improved property at full market value, typically within 2–4 months. Profit is split per the pre-agreed JV terms.

Capital Protection

Four Layers Protecting Your Capital

We've built multiple safeguards into our model — so your capital is never exposed to avoidable errors, fraud, or opacity.

  1. Title-Verified Assets

    Every property undergoes a full title check at the Land Registry with a qualified lawyer before capital is committed.

  2. Written JV Agreement

    A lawyer-drafted agreement defines capital, profit split, timeline, exit terms, and dispute resolution before money changes hands.

  3. Full Transparency

    You receive a complete Deal Analyzer before you invest — no hidden fees. You approve each deal individually.

  4. Start Small, Build Trust

    Begin with a single deal to watch the acquisition-renovation-resale cycle play out. Scale only when you're confident.
